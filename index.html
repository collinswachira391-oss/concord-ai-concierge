05 · Lead recovery system

Don't let interested patients disappear.

A lead recovery layer can detect incomplete journeys and trigger approved reminders or staff tasks. It is designed to recover attention, not pressure patients.

06 · The opportunity

From clinic website to care platform.

TFMC's broad service mix creates many possible entry points. This concept turns that breadth into a single navigable system: patients get a simpler front door, staff get structured context, and organisations get dedicated routes into TFMC programmes.

Patient experienceOne “what do you need?” starting point across appointments, lab, pharmacy, telemedicine and support.
Staff operationsA command view for enquiries, pending actions, demand signals and recovery tasks.
Corporate growthA purpose-built route for employers, screenings, wellness and recurring programmes.
School & community growthA structured route for school health days, screening and outreach programmes.
Prepared for Tim's Family Medical Clinic

One front door. Many care journeys.

This is a product concept, not a claim that these integrations are already live. The next step would be to map TFMC's real workflows, approved communication channels and systems, then prototype the highest-value journey first.
